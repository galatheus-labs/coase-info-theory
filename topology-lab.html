App 1 · Topology lab

Compare organizational topologies under the same environment.

This is the cleanest visual for the paper’s core claim: organizations differ in how much decision-relevant information they can preserve while the world is changing.

Interpretation

Move the sliders. As volatility and interdependence rise, the organization needs more information-processing capacity. Better protocols, observability, and software agents increase that capacity and reduce distortion.

Agility is treated as a proxy for how quickly the system turns world-state information into aligned action.
