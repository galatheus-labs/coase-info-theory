Formal note structure

How the demos map to the paper

  • Definitions: world state X_t, local observations Y_t^i, messages M_t^{ij}, actions A_t^i.
  • Agility functional: use a proxy like A_g = I(X_t;A_t)/(τ_t·C_t).
  • Proposition 1: better protocols increase usable information flow and reduce distortion.
  • Proposition 2: agent nodes reduce sensing-to-action latency for structured tasks.
  • Proposition 3: the efficient firm boundary shifts when machine-readable coordination improves.
Reality anchors

  • Customer support triage and escalation routing

  • Vendor outage coordination and compliance-heavy workflows
