Connect the theory to real workflows that already feel painful.

Incidents reveal organizational agility. They make delay, distortion, and brittle handoffs visible in a way that abstract cost functions do not.

Why this matters

If the paper says agents improve agility, readers should see a concrete before/after: faster detection, fewer handoffs, better routing, and a tighter loop from signal to coordinated action.

Best framing: a deployment incident is a dynamic control problem under uncertainty.
