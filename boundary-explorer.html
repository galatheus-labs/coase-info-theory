App 3 · Boundary explorer

Show how software agents shift the efficient boundary of the firm.

This is the explicit Coase app. It compares three organizational choices: keep the work in-house, outsource it to the market, or coordinate it through an agent-mediated hybrid boundary.

Interpretation

The key move is not merely “transaction costs fall.” The deeper claim is that machine-readable coordination reduces communication, interpretation, monitoring, and delay costs across boundaries.

The hybrid option is where the paper becomes distinctively modern.

Why this matters

The firm boundary depends on search, contract, communication, interpretation, monitoring, delay, and error. Agents matter because they can turn more of those terms into programmable information flows.

Strong paper sentence: agents create a new region between hierarchy and market, not just a cheaper version of one or the other.
